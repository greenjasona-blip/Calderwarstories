The series

For twenty years, every portrayal of Special Forces on screen has been sanitized. Hero-worshipped. Politicized. The Bushmen is the corrective — built on nine combat deployments of lived experience, told with the brutal honesty the subject demands.

Generation Kill meets The Bear — unsanitized military realism, intimate character drama, dark humor, and the emotional cost of brotherhood.

Season One

13 episodes · Afghanistan, 2013 – 2015

Thirteen episodes. Two deployment cycles. The season opens with a green-on-blue attack at VSO Jalrez — objective camera, no score, just the truth — and ends with Jason on a HLZ at Bagram, coming home.

01
Jalrez — The PilotComplete

Green-on-blue attack. Objective camera. No score. Jay arrives, reports to SGM, meets ODA 36. The sandbag throne. The Mason story. The fire pit. He is home.

02
34 / 35Complete

George Bannar KIA in Wardak. Chuck codes twice, survives. The Shank memorial.

03
The New Guy

Funeral detail. Lindsey: “Come back to me.” The DUI story. The acid trip.

04
Welcome to Kunduz

Abandoned airbase. Chinook insertion. First kill — handled with restraint.

05
The Dirty SideComplete

“Fancy” by Iggy Azalea. Wrong-side insertion. Chuck shot. Danger-close 500lb bomb.

06
The Last Night in Kunduz

RPG ambush. JJ roof circus. The last fist pound with Mikey Cathcart.

07
The Ones Who Come Home

Pitch Perfect — last pure lightness. CIB awarding. Civilian casualty. Moral center.

08
NangarharComplete

Thanksgiving. Equipment prep ritual. The contact. Blunt force. Sky spinning. Cut to black.

09
The Coin Has a SayComplete

ISR: perfect L-shaped ambush. Airstrike denied. “Get off my fingers.” “Treat yourself.”

10
The Waiting Room

Lindsey’s side of the call. Bear-style pressure cooker. Dan calls. Fort Bragg reunion.

11
The Quiet

Jay’s dissociation at home. The boys still fighting in Nangarhar. “Jay… come back to me.”

12
The Mountain

Jay absent entirely. Reggie’s baptism by fire with 35. Ray: “Welcome to the show, kid.”

13
The Return

German transit. Bagram HLZ. JJ: “Why so serious?” He is home. He is whole.
